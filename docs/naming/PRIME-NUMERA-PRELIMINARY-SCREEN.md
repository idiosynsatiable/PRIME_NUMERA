# PRIME NUMERA Preliminary Commercial-Name Screen

Date: 2026-09-26  
Status: **working codename only**  
Purpose: preliminary product-name risk screening. This is not a trademark clearance opinion or legal advice.

## Executive finding

No obvious exact-match product named **PRIME NUMERA** surfaced in the limited indexed-web searches performed for this screen.

However, **NUMERA is already crowded and directly occupied in the numerology-app category**. That is materially more important than an exact-wording search because the proposed product would operate in a closely overlapping field.

Commercial adoption of PRIME NUMERA therefore remains an explicit legal/naming gate.

## Required gate before commercial adoption

Before PRIME NUMERA changes from working codename to commercial product name:

1. Run a comprehensive USPTO trademark search, including exact, phonetic, spelling, and confusingly similar variants.
2. Review relevant Nice classes for downloadable software, SaaS, education/entertainment, and related services.
3. Review state/common-law and general web uses, not only federal registrations.
4. Review Apple App Store and Google Play naming conflicts again immediately before adoption.
5. Verify and acquire desired domains/social handles before public naming.
6. Have qualified trademark counsel review the collision set if the owner intends to invest materially in the mark.
