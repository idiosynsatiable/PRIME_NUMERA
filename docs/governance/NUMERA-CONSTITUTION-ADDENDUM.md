# PRIME NUMERA constitutional addendum

Date: 2026-09-27 (America/Chicago). This codifies the existing NUMERA rules and complements the PRIME Operating Constitution v2.0; it does not replace owner authority, platform permissions or accepted repository ADRs.

1. Deterministic calculations and published fixtures are authoritative. Keep interpretation, cultural research and presentation outside arithmetic.
2. Preserve distinct Pythagorean and Chaldean/Cheiro methodology, explicit normalization and reduction policies, provenance and versioning.
3. Separate historical fact, traditional meaning, modern interpretation and entertainment. Never invent scientific validity, rarity, predictions or compatibility percentages.
4. Keep names, dates and raw derivations out of public payloads, analytics and logs. Local calculation is available without an account; saving and synchronization require deliberate consent.
5. Shares disclose only approved fields. Use cryptographic opaque IDs, runtime allowlists, explicit visibility, verified ownership, expiration and revocation. Never serialize owner metadata into a public page or card. A user-selected public label may identify them; do not claim it is anonymous.
6. Require server-derived identity at private read, update, export and delete boundaries. Client-supplied owner identifiers are untrusted.
7. Preserve cultural source context and living traditions. Modern creative activities are not historical evidence.
8. Preserve ADR 0002: obtain approval of concrete concepts before final UI implementation. Non-visual foundations continue independently. Graphs require accessible structured alternatives; core workflows support mobile, keyboard, reduced motion and offline use.
9. Preserve reproducible builds, migrations, tests, provenance and rollback. A live landing page is not the completed application. Verify the actual user journey on the actual deployed revision.
10. Continue reversible authorized engineering without repeated approvals. Preserve the existing production, spending, credentials, destructive-data, commercial-naming and design gates. Prior approval applies only to its established scope.
11. Use truthful status: implemented, tested, integrated, deployed and production-proven are different. Record failures and remaining work without claiming automatic continuation after a session ends.
12. Report one constitutional outcome: VERIFIED DELIVERY, CONDITIONAL DELIVERY, OPERATOR ACTION REQUIRED or BUILD FAILED. Required features remain incomplete until their acceptance evidence exists.
