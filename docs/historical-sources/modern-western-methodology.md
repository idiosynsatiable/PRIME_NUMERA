### Balance

Title: Numerology Balance Number  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-balance-number/

Method detail relevant to PRIME NUMERA:
- sum the values of the initials of the full birth name
- reduce the result to a single digit
- Master Numbers are not preserved in this method

### Planes of Expression

Title: Numerology Planes of Expression  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-articles/planes-of-expression.html

Method detail relevant to PRIME NUMERA:
- Physical letters: D, E, M, W
- Mental letters: A, G, H, J, L, N, P
- Emotional letters: B, I, O, R, S, T, X, Z
- Intuitive letters: C, F, K, Q, U, V, Y
- both letter counts and mapped values can be shown

### Transits

Title: Transits  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-articles/numerology-transits.html

Method detail relevant to PRIME NUMERA:
- name letters define Transit cycles
- each letter lasts a number of years equal to its mapped value
- the sequence starts at age 0
- the name sequence repeats after its total duration
- first, middle, and last name groups are used as separate Transit channels in the documented three-name convention

### Essence Cycles

Title: Essence Cycles  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-articles/essence-cycles.html

Method detail relevant to PRIME NUMERA:
- the Essence value for an age is the sum of the active Transit values
- preserving the compound total separately from the reduced value is useful for transparent calculation display

## Historical warning

The presence of labels such as “Pythagorean” or “Chaldean” in modern numerology does not, by itself, establish direct continuity with ancient Pythagorean mathematics or ancient Mesopotamian divination. Those historical questions require separate primary/academic source review.
