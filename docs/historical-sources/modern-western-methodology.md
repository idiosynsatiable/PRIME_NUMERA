### Expression and component reduction

Title: Expression Number – Your Natural Talents & Life Goal  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-expression/

Method details used by PRIME NUMERA:
- calculate first, middle, and last birth-name components separately
- reduce each component before the final addition
- preserve 11, 22, and 33 when encountered
- retain component and final compound totals because they can carry additional methodology labels

Published worked fixture:
- Thomas = 22
- Cruise = 30 → 3
- Mapother = 42 → 6
- 22 + 3 + 6 = 31 → 4

PRIME NUMERA tests this fixture directly.

### Karmic Debt

Title: Karmic Debt Numbers  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-karmic-debt-numbers/

Method details used by PRIME NUMERA:
- Karmic Debt compound numbers are 13, 14, 16, and 19
- they are significant when found in core numbers including Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, Personality, and Birth Day
- PRIME NUMERA records the exact arithmetic stage that produced a debt number instead of scanning unrelated numbers and attaching significance afterward

For the Decoz-style core-name calculations, PRIME NUMERA requires the component-aware strategy before deriving a Karmic Debt label.

### Karmic Lessons, Hidden Passion, and Subconscious Self

Numerology Course Part 2  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-course-classes/classes/numerology-workshop-part-2.pdf

Method details:
- Karmic Lessons are values 1 through 9 missing from the full birth name
- Hidden Passion is the most frequently represented value; ties are possible
- Subconscious Self can be expressed as the count of represented values, equivalently 9 minus the number of Karmic Lessons

These labels are applied only to the Pythagorean one-through-nine mapping in the current engine.

### Balance

Title: Numerology Balance Number  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-balance-number/

Method details:
- sum the values of the initials of the full birth name
- reduce the result to a single digit
- Master Numbers are not preserved in this method

### Bridge Numbers

Title: Numerology Bridge Numbers  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-bridge-numbers/

Method details:
- a Bridge is the absolute difference between two named core numbers
- documented examples include Life Path / Expression and Heart's Desire / Personality
- PRIME NUMERA stores the bridge kind rather than returning an unlabeled difference

### Long-term cycles

General calculation reference:
https://www.worldnumerology.com/do-your-own-reading/

Method details:
- the first Pinnacle and First Period transition at age 36 minus the single-digit Life Path
- second and third Pinnacles last nine years each
- the second Period lasts 27 years
- the third Period lasts for the remainder of life
- Challenges do not use the same hard cycle boundaries as the Pinnacles

### Transits

Title: Transits  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-articles/numerology-transits.html

Method details:
- name letters define Transit cycles
- each letter lasts a number of years equal to its mapped value
- the sequence starts at age 0
- the name sequence repeats after its total duration
- first, middle, and last name groups are used as separate Transit channels in the documented three-name convention

### Essence Cycles

Title: Essence Cycles  
URL: https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-articles/essence-cycles.html

Method details:
- the Essence value for an age is the sum of the active Transit values
- preserving the compound total separately from the reduced value is useful for transparent calculation display

## Historical warning

The presence of labels such as “Pythagorean” or “Chaldean” in modern numerology does not, by itself, establish direct continuity with ancient Pythagorean mathematics or ancient Mesopotamian divination. Those historical questions require separate primary/academic source review.
