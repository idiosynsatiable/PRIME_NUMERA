# Chaldean / Cheiro-Style Numerology Provenance

Classification: modern Western esoteric numerology methodology with a documented publication trail; **not** treated as proven ancient Mesopotamian continuity.

## Bibliographic anchor

Cheiro, *Cheiro's Book of Numbers*.

Open Library record: https://openlibrary.org/books/OL5909902M/Cheiro%27s_book_of_numbers  
Google Books record for a 1927 edition: https://books.google.com/books/about/Cheiro_s_Book_of_Numbers.html?id=-qMgZzxmN1oC

These records establish the existence and publication history of Cheiro's work. They do not, by themselves, prove that its Latin-letter table was used by ancient Chaldeans.

## Engine consequence

PRIME NUMERA labels the engine **Chaldean / Cheiro-style**.

It exposes the compound name total separately from the reduced result and does not automatically import the Pythagorean 11/22/33 Master Number preservation rule into the Chaldean reducer.

## Historical caution

Claims such as “ancient Babylonian practitioners used this exact English alphabet table” require evidence that is not supplied by the modern numerology sources above. PRIME NUMERA therefore treats those claims as unverified rather than repeating them as historical fact.
