# PRIME NUMERA

**Working codename. Commercial naming remains subject to trademark, domain, app-store, and naming research.**

PRIME NUMERA is a transparent, interactive numerology and symbolic-systems platform featuring auditable calculations, Number DNA visualization, name analysis, timelines, compatibility, and historically sourced cultural exploration.

## Product principles

- Calculations first, interpretations second.
- Keep mathematical systems distinct.
- Separate arithmetic, history, traditional interpretation, modern interpretation, and entertainment-oriented material.
- Treat cultural and historical systems independently and source them responsibly.
- Mobile-first, accessible, performant, privacy-conscious, and offline-capable at the core.
- Never manufacture numerical depth or statistical rarity.

## Development status

Foundation/bootstrap phase. Features are not considered complete until implemented, tested, visually verified where applicable, documented, security/performance checked, and supported by CI evidence.
